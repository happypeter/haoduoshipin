Hello, Peter here, today we r gona build a rabbit hutch. I have several design goals.

- Firstly, it will be two parts, a warm cabinet, and a airy conpartment. Airflow is key in summer.
- Secondly, setting sunlight will be kept away, again, rabbits r very coldness tolerent, but not hotness.
- Thirdly, no poop/urine will stay inside, a floor without wood support.
- fourthly, wire cloth mount inside, so that rabbit can not chew on wood.

All said, just wanna show I v done my homework, a dozen youtube videos been watched, and there r other design concerns, like how to clean the poop and so on, which we will show along the way.

## Primary lumber

the size of the framing lumber will mostly 4by4, after checking many designs online, this seems to be a common trick to put together small structures that do not require a whole lot stength. very easy to screw together, and to add more pieces to the easisting structure. anywhere you need more strength, do the sheathing or a bit bracing.
